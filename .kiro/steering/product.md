# Marrow

Marrow gives reviewers a way to trace a proposed conclusion back to the material they supplied. Postmortem Court uses individual log and diff lines to explain an outage. Defense Tribunal uses manuscript passages to build a decision letter and a revision checklist. Both run in the browser.

Keep the example inputs synthetic. Model-generated conclusions are interpretations of those inputs. Show the cited exhibit or passage alongside each conclusion so readers can judge it for themselves.
